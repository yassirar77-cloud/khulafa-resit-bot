# Codebase audit — July 2026

Five parallel reviews (bot core, ingestion/parsing, kitchen/orders,
analytics/reconciliation, security/hygiene) over the whole repo. Everything
below was verified against the code before being acted on. The **Fixed**
section shipped in this branch; the **Known issues** section needs an owner
decision or a schema change and was deliberately left alone.

## Fixed in this branch

Security / authorization
- `/advances` had no reviewer gate: any Telegram user could read every
  outlet's staff-loan ledger and mark advances repaid. Command and the Y/N
  confirmation reply are now `is_reviewer`-gated.
- GitHub Actions `ingest-latency-check`: `workflow_dispatch` inputs were
  interpolated straight into the shell (script injection); now passed via
  env. Added `permissions: {}`.

Correctness — receipt pipeline
- Verifier **date corrections were silently discarded**: `receipt_date` was
  copied from `date` before verification and won at insert time.
  `_apply_corrections` now keeps both in sync.
- Two reviewers tapping “Save as-is” on the same pending item could both
  promote it (duplicate receipts). Finalization now claims the row with a
  compare-and-swap on `status='pending'` before promoting, and rolls the
  claim back if the insert fails.
- `_check_big_purchase` included the receipt being audited in its own
  baseline, suppressing alerts near the 2x threshold.
- Replies longer than Telegram's 4096-char limit (many-item receipts,
  `/advances` lists) crashed the handler mid-pipeline *after* the receipt
  was stored, skipping side-table routing/price aggregation/audit checks.
  Replies are now chunked and non-fatal; digest `pack_messages` hard-splits
  a single oversized section instead of aborting the rest of the digest.
- Callback buttons older than 48h arrive as `InaccessibleMessage` (no
  `reply_text`) — confirmations after Save/Discard/apply-all now fall back
  to a plain `send_message` instead of raising after the DB write.
- `/advances <name>` misrouted 2–5-letter staff names (DINA, ALI…) to the
  outlet filter and replied “tiada advance outstanding” for staff who owe.
  Outlet-shaped tokens now fall through to staff history when no advance
  rows carry that outlet.

Correctness — PostgREST 1000-row cap (silent truncation)
Hosted Supabase clamps every request (including big `.limit()`s) to
~1000 rows. Added `db_pagination.fetch_all_pages` and applied it to:
- `/reparse_apply_all` and `/backfill_apply_all` (reported “applied ALL”
  after applying only the first 1000)
- `order_generator.fetch_item_price_rows` (nightly order drafts computed
  from an arbitrary 1000-row slice of 90-day history)
- `backfill_items` / `backfill_canonical` fetchers (backfills permanently
  re-read the same first 1000 receipts; newer receipts never resolved)
- `digest_data`: price-movements window, data-quality counters,
  dead-letter scan; `merchant_auto_resolve` backlog;
  `sales_ingest.load_error_counts`; `merchant_resolver`/`item_resolver`
  snapshots; `config/kitchen_groups` receipts map

Correctness — kitchen logging
- Double-tap of **Hantar** ran `finalize_submission` twice concurrently;
  the additive night phase double-added cooked quantities. Submission now
  claims the session open→submitting via CAS (with stale-claim recovery so
  a crash mid-submit can't strand the form).
- A transient DB failure during group resolution was cached as an empty
  map forever, silencing all kitchen forms until restart. Failures are no
  longer cached.

Correctness — sales ingestion
- Shift closes between 23:00–04:59 were classified `unknown` and dated to
  the wrong business day (production fixture: a 00:09 close the D-file
  books to the previous day). 00:00–10:59 now folds back as overnight;
  17:00–23:59 is the day shift.
- IMAP used message **sequence numbers** where UIDs are required — another
  client expunging from the shared inbox mid-batch could mark the wrong
  email `\Seen`, silently dropping a shift forever. Switched to
  UID SEARCH/FETCH/STORE.
- `parse_datetime` rejected full month names (`26/June/2026 …` — every
  fixture is May, the one month whose full name equals its abbreviation),
  falling back to “today” for the D-file business date. Added `%B` format
  and tolerate `7:00:04PM` (no space) which the MOBILE CASH regex accepts.
- `normalize_total`: `"13,5"`/`"13,50"` were read as 135/1350 (10x
  inflation); commas are now validated as grouping vs decimal, ambiguous
  shapes return None (routes to review). Refund shapes `-RM13.00` and
  accounting `(13.00)` now parse as negative instead of None.

Digest accuracy
- PRICE ALERTS compared an 8-day “recent” window to a 7-day prior one
  (header claims 7v7); now 7v7 adjacent windows.
- The fallback TOP SUPPLIERS path counts receipts, not item lines — the
  label now says “receipts” on that path.

Hygiene
- Untracked the four committed `date_repair_journal_*.jsonl` artifacts
  (already gitignored, ignore rule postdated the commit).
- `tests/test_repair_corrupt_dates.py` wrote journal files into the repo
  working tree on every run; they now go to a temp dir.

## Known issues — need an owner decision (not changed)

1. **`items[].price` means two different things.** `price_aggregation.py`
   (and its tests) treat it as the *unit* price; `analytics.py` and the
   `price_movements` SQL view (and their tests) treat it as the *line
   total*. For a line `qty 7 × 6.30` one stack records RM44.10 @ 6.30/unit,
   the other RM6.30 @ 0.90/unit. Every digest top-items/supplier figure and
   the spike detector inherit whichever is wrong. Decide the real OCR
   semantics (check a few receipts), then align the other stack + view.
2. **`/webapp` dashboard is unauthenticated.** It ships `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`
   to any visitor and never validates Telegram WebApp `initData` (HMAC).
   Safety currently rests entirely on Supabase RLS for the `receipts`
   table. Verify RLS denies the anon role, or add server-side initData
   verification.
3. **Food-cost rolling window bias**: the 7-day window includes the digest
   day, whose sales are structurally absent at 23:00 — the headline % is
   inflated ~×7/6 nightly and can falsely print 🔴 INVESTIGATE. Options:
   end the window at yesterday, or skip days with NULL sales.
4. **Receipts uploaded >7 days late are never reconciled** (the nightly job
   only re-runs the last `FOOD_COST_LOOKBACK_DAYS = 7` dates), silently
   removing that spend from food cost. Consider widening lookback or
   triggering reconciliation for the receipt's business date at upload.
5. **Evening COOKED form submitted after the night form** overwrites the
   additive total (50 clobbers 20+50=70) and zeroes night-only items.
   Needs an ordering rule (block cooked-after-night, or make cooked a
   partial update once a night row exists).
6. **Numpad buffers are per-user**: user A types digits, user B taps ✓ —
   commits nothing and wipes A's buffer. Also concurrent ✓ on different
   items can lose one item (entries JSON read-modify-write). Fix needs
   per-item DB-side merge (e.g. jsonb patch) rather than whole-dict save.
7. **Daily summary blind spot 23:00–24:00**: the 23:00 job covers the full
   calendar day, so receipts logged 23:00–24:00 are in no summary ever.
   Move the job to 23:59, or make the window “since the last run”.
8. **Promoted review receipts lose their outlet** (`derive_outlet(chat_id,
   None)` with the hardcoded-empty `GROUP_OUTLET_MAP` is always None).
   Store the outlet on the pending_review row at queue time (schema add).
9. **`normalize_date` rewrites any pre-2024 ISO year to hardcoded 2026**
   (a 2023 receipt becomes a future date; constant goes stale in 2027),
   and the same bump is *not* applied to DD/MM/YYYY input — the identical
   date normalizes differently depending on the OCR's format.
10. **Merchant fuzzy tiers overmatch short names**: single generic-word
    canonicals (MEWAH, REZA, SAIDA) auto-resolve unrelated merchants at
    confidence ≥85, and the Levenshtein≤5 tier maps SHELL→SAIDA, LOTUS→
    BABAS at conf 60 — which also hides genuinely new suppliers from the
    digest. Consider length-aware distance and a stoplist for generic words.
11. **Partial ingest is permanent**: if the summary row inserts but a child
    (itemwise/payouts) insert fails, the retry sees the parent and skips as
    “duplicate”, and the email is marked read. Dedup should verify children
    (or save should be transactional via an RPC).
12. **NULL `shift_no` defeats the unique constraint** (NULLs are distinct
    in Postgres): overlapping manual+scheduled polls can double-insert, and
    two same-day shifts that both lose their number collide in the app-side
    check, losing the second one. Consider a partial unique index /
    sentinel value.
13. **Review-queue dedup and registration-code burn are check-then-act**
    (no DB constraint); rare double DM/duplicate queue rows, and a
    registration code can be redeemed twice concurrently. `/register` also
    binds report delivery to whatever chat it's typed in — a manager doing
    it in the staff group leaks weekly financials to the group.
14. **`skipped_unknown` emails are re-fetched and re-logged every poll**
    with no dead-letter cap — unbounded `sales_ingest_log` growth from one
    unregistered outlet.
